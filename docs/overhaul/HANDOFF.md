## 1. What changed, by journey

**Prospect reads the site (public, EN + AR).** One typed catalog (`src/lib/catalog/index.ts`) drives the home page (four audience entry points, five services, process, FAQ teaser), `/services`, one page per service (`/services/<slug>`: intended client, what we shoot, explicit photo/video deliverables incl. full match vs highlights vs reels vs raw, what we need, booking and payment steps, permitted use, limitations, related FAQs, request CTA), `/how-it-works` (process + searchable categorised FAQ, 46 answers), `/work` (indexable showcase with an accessible lightbox; `/portfolio` 308→`/work`; "Find my photos" stays the separate external gallery link) and `/contact`. Arabic is natural copy, not a translation of the layout: `?lang=ar` sets the `bbm_lang` cookie and redirects to the same URL; `<html lang dir="rtl">` applies on public routes only. Design tokens, type scale, buttons, inputs, chips, badges, dialog, table and figure styles live in `src/app/globals.css`; focus rings, 44 px targets, reduced motion and logical (RTL-safe) properties throughout. SEO: sitemap with service pages, robots disallow for every private route, Service/Breadcrumb/FAQ JSON-LD without invented prices or reviews, canonical from `siteUrl()`. "Set your price." placeholder sentences are stripped on public read and no longer seeded. Testimonials render only when the owner published them with permission; the "share your experience" sales-section empty state is gone.

**Prospect requests coverage (`/book`).** One progressive flow: what (service, media scope, explicit video deliverables, optional package) → the day (per-service fields; roster/athlete count, coverage hours, deadline, intended use, access/privacy needs, optional budget with "Not sure"; bracket/time may come later) → about you (client type, contact, under-18 guardian + consent) → review and consents. Error summary with focus jumps, values preserved on back/validation, legacy `?type=` and `?kind=` links keep working and preselect. The client payload carries `price_label`/`quote` only, never `price_qr`. Submission is one transaction in the SECURITY DEFINER RPC `photo_submit_public_booking` (service role only): person by e-mail (never rewriting an existing person's contact details), organisation, lead, booking with generated reference, lead↔booking link, audit rows; idempotency key replay returns the same booking and re-enqueues the (alert-key idempotent) notices. **Price-mode bug fixed**: `bookableAmount()` is the single decision; a `quote`-mode package or an always-quoted service yields amount 0 / `quote` / `inquiry`. After the RPC: owner Telegram, client e-mail, private pricing assessment. `/book/done` verifies the reference exists and states "not a confirmed reservation".

**Owner answers a lead and prices it (private, owner only).** `/leads` shows an assessment badge; `/bookings/<id>` shows the next action with due date, capacity warnings (same-day confirmed bookings/events), the shoot brief panel and the assessment state. Pricing intelligence (`src/lib/pricing/engine`) produces three separate outputs per versioned snapshot: market comparison (comparable scope only, exclusions and confidence visible, Qatar vs GCC separate), sustainable cost floor (each line once; full-match vs highlights vs reels costed differently; crew from mats and roster), recommendation low/target/high with `price = cost / (1 − margin − fee)` reconciled against the market range, infeasible/unprofitable flags, team total + effective per-athlete. Evidence lives in `photo_price_references` with observation date, source type, confidence, region, scope fields and a `superseded_by` history; 20 Qatar observations (15 Oct 2026) ship as a seed to import, with the search-index and low-confidence rows marked "needs re-verification". `POST /api/cron/pricing` processes queued assessments and reports research as `disabled`/`not_configured` honestly (no provider exists; nothing is browsed). Only "Issue quote" sets `issued_at`; nothing is e-mailed from pricing.

**Owner runs the business.** Navigation regrouped: Overview, Sales, Production, Tournament day, Finance & delivery, Website, Settings & integrations; phone bar = Today, Watcher, Leads, Bookings, More. `/studio` overview (priorities, requests needing action, forecast vs collected clearly labelled, delivery work, incidents, open questions, `?q=` universal search), `/studio/metrics`, `/production` board derived from existing states (only culling/editing/review stored in `details.production`), `/bookings/<id>/brief` (printable shoot brief + client checklist with due dates), `/website/testimonials` (permission + published flags), `/website/faqs` (owner overrides merged into the public FAQ) and open-questions tracking.

**Owner manages portfolio media.** `/media`: batch drag/drop or picker, SHA-256 duplicate detection, per-file progress, validation (decoded format vs claimed mime, size, dimensions, no SVG/GIF/animated), EXIF/GPS/ICC stripped, responsive derivatives + blur, alt/caption/category/tags/focal point, placements (hero, featured, work, per service), rights/guardian confirmation gates publishing, unpublish and recoverable archive, storage usage. Originals and unpublished derivatives live in the private bucket; publishing copies derivatives to the public bucket, unpublishing removes them; studio preview uses short-lived signed URLs. CLI import (`npm run photos:import -- --source <dir> --dry-run|--limit|--resume|--suggest-placements|--vision --vision-cap`) writes to the same library as `ready`, never publishes, never touches originals.
